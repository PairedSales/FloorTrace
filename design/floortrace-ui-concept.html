The pipeline is visible

Plan ▸ Scale ▸ Outline ▸ Report is the app's real state machine. It now has a place on screen that shows what is done and what needs attention.

Was: twenty flat buttons, no sense of order or progress.

Mode lives where you work

Picking a tool opens a context bar with its options and explicit Done / Cancel, and names the mode in the status bar.

Was: eight permanent toasts floating over the plan as the only mode indicator.

Doubt reads as loudly as the number

Confidence is a chip on the outline; reasons are full-size prose with a Show button. Segment anchors highlight every doubtful run at once — a bridged opening is a gap across the outline, not a dot.

Report-level findings — a floor counted twice, a void the outline moved out from under — sit on the total they qualify.

Was: 9px text at 2.99:1, an 8px "notes" link at 1.88:1, and the double-count warning at 9px amber.

The plan is never covered

Chrome docks to the edges — measurement panel left, 48px tool rail right — replacing two floating columns that both sat on the drawing's top-left corner.

Was: ~362 × 360px of panel permanently over the artwork.

A themed work surface

The plan is a sheet on a neutral surface that follows the theme — the drafting-table convention. Overlay hues are reserved for the drawing and never reused as UI state.

Was: a hard #ffffff viewport beside #21222C chrome — a 15.8:1 step at the panel edge.

Desktop conventions, kept

Menus, 32px commands, a status bar carrying mode, scale, cursor and zoom, tooltips that show their keybinding, and a 12px type floor.

Was: 28px commands, no zoom readout, no status bar, 34 native title tooltips as the only documentation.
